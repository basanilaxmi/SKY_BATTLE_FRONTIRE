SKYFALL: BATTLE FRONTIER — Prototype 0.1

HOW TO RUN
1. Extract SKYFALL_BATTLE_FRONTIER.zip.
2. Open index.html in a modern browser with internet access.
3. Three.js is loaded from jsDelivr, so the first launch needs an internet connection.
4. Tap ENTER TRAINING.
5. On mobile: use the left joystick to move, drag the right side to turn the camera, and tap FIRE to shoot.
   On desktop: WASD/arrow keys to move, drag the scene to look, Space to jump, click FIRE to shoot.

INCLUDED
- Original low-poly 3D training island and buildings
- Third-person character and target bots
- Touch joystick, jump, aim, fire
- Camera/red dot/2x/4x/sniper sensitivity sliders
- Local sensitivity settings
- Target hits and elimination counter

PROTOTYPE LIMITATIONS
This is an early solo training prototype, not a finished commercial game. It does not yet include online multiplayer, server matchmaking, real character skeletal animation, a full battle royale safe zone, vehicle driving, pets, parachuting, or a complete weapon inventory. These are next development stages. Graphics and performance depend on your device/browser.
